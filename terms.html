What you get

4. What Joru does — and what it does not

Joru builds day-by-day travel itineraries. Places, area guides and weather come from open data; the budget, sequence and transport estimates are computed on your device; an AI model that runs on your device arranges the day and writes the guide.

Joru is a planning tool, not a travel agency. It does not sell, book, reserve or guarantee any flight, stay, ticket, tour or table. Any booking you make happens with a third party, under that third party's own terms.

Plans are AI-generated

Itineraries are drafted by an AI model and may contain errors: an opening time may have changed, a price may be out of date, a place may have closed. Joru is not professional travel advice. Always verify opening hours, prices, availability, entry requirements and local safety information before you travel or spend money. Prices and budgets shown in the app are estimates.

Free & Premium

5. Plans, billing and cancellation

Planning is free and requires no account. Because the AI runs on your device, generating a plan costs nothing to run.

Free includes: one destination per trip, trips of up to 5 days, real places, weather and area guides, a day-by-day itinerary you can edit, a budget estimate with real-spend tracking, the interactive map, the packing list, background generation, and export to PDF, Markdown or your calendar.

Premium adds: trips longer than 5 days, multiple destinations in one trip, a detailed budget by category, concrete restaurants filtered by cuisine, diet and price, transport between cities and day-trip excursions, and encrypted sync to your own cloud storage (Google Drive on Android, iCloud on iOS).
